# Premium Corporate Website Delivery Standard

## Experience requirements

A corporate website must communicate positioning, credibility, services, evidence, and a clear next action. Visual polish must support comprehension and trust rather than compete with them.

## Required discovery outputs

- Business objective and measurable conversion goal.
- Primary and secondary audience definitions.
- Value proposition and proof points.
- Competitor/reference analysis supplied or approved by the project owner.
- Required pages, languages, legal notices, and integrations.
- Content owner and approval path.

## Required design outputs

- Sitemap and navigation model.
- Design principles and visual direction.
- Color, typography, spacing, grid, elevation, border, and motion tokens.
- Component inventory with variants and states.
- Responsive behavior for each major component.
- Approved imagery, iconography, and illustration rules.
- Accessibility annotations and interaction specifications.

## Required page patterns

Select based on business requirements; do not force every pattern into every site.

- Home / positioning.
- Company / leadership / credibility.
- Products or services.
- Case studies, proof, or portfolio.
- Insights / news / resources.
- Contact / lead capture.
- Privacy, terms, and other required legal pages.

## Content model

Keep content separate from layout where practical. Define schemas for repeated entities such as services, people, case studies, articles, FAQs, and navigation items. Validate required fields and metadata before publication.

## Visual quality criteria

- Strong typographic hierarchy and controlled line lengths.
- Consistent alignment, spacing, and section rhythm.
- Intentional image art direction and responsive crops.
- Distinctive but restrained interaction and motion.
- Coherent component behavior across all pages.
- No generic template styling unless explicitly selected.
- No decorative visual that reduces legibility or conversion clarity.

## Acceptance

The site is accepted only when the owner approves the content and visual direction, required journeys work across supported devices, quality gates pass, and the release record is complete.
